Scores and priority buckets are derived arithmetically from the rubric. A category scoring 10 means no mechanical violation was measured in it, not that a human declared it perfect. Spec §8 describes the layers; §9 lists exactly which rules fall where.

⚠ Score levels are not comparable with the previous period
The 2026-08 row folds a judgment overlay into 348 of 348 lectures; 2026-05 folded one into 0 of 300. A lecture assessed against more rules scores lower — the published mean moving 8.2 → 7.7 is the judgment layer landing on one period, not the lectures changing. Like for like (evidence layer alone, history_mechanical.csv): 8.2 → 8.4 overall, Writing 6.6 → 7.1, HIGH 102 → 85. The trend above is therefore reported on rule reach, never on published scores across a coverage change.
